# Code of Conduct

Paperlight follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome attention, in any space of the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
([@webKing021](https://github.com/webKing021)). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions, or ban participants, who break
this code.
